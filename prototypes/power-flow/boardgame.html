Throwaway prototype · board-game framing

DTPR, as a board game

The play mat has permanent, labelled regions. You deal a card into each slot — one card per DTPR element — then drop coloured chips on the cards to show the loaded stuff: who identifies whom (PII), and how much the machine decides (autonomy).

The schema already thinks like a board game: every category has a shape (hexagon, circle, rounded-square, octagon) and the touchy attributes are already coloured discriminators — autonomy is teal/purple/orange, PII is blue/purple/gold. So the pieces here are the real DTPR components, not a metaphor bolted on.

Card shapes = category families

Pieces map straight to ai@2026-05-06-beta: card shapes are the category shape field; the autonomy dial is the functional_modes.autonomy context; PII chips are the input/output_dataset.pii context; the role ribbon is accountable.role.
