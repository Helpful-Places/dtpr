DTPR Power Flow

What if a datachain weren't a flat list of tiles, but a small graph of typed relationships — showing not just who a system is done for, but who it's done to, and who answers to whom?

The linear chain stays the default, glanceable read. The power flow is an opt-in deep view that adds the one thing a list can't hold: the edges. It reads left-to-right like a sentence, and it makes the accountability gap — affects the public, but answers only to the vendor — visible at a glance.
Prototype mirrors @dtpr/ui tokens (teal #0f5153, Red Hat Text, warning #f04a4a) and DTPR's hexagon-tile motif. Edge verbs and the affects/accountable-to split follow the research corpus entry relational-accountability-graph-model (Bovens' actor→forum; ODRL deontic can edges; Ghoniem's "keep it a short path"). Next step would be a real <DtprPowerFlow> component in packages/ui.
