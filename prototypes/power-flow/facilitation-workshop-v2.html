DTPR Agreement Workshop

Rebuilt around the principle that makes deliberation work: diverge, then converge. The room first surfaces every concern against the disclosed system (on the wall), clusters them into a few named tension points, deliberates redesigns — and only then registers a gradient of agreement, once, on the resulting conditions. No voting on raw DTPR categories.

Four print pieces. 1 · Run-of-show (facilitator script, Letter). 2 · Wall sheets — one per disclosed seat, for surfacing concerns (Letter, print 5). 3 · Convergence mat — the big 17×11 tabloid / A3 artifact where tension points get their gradient and the outcome lands. 4 · Tokens & red-lines to cut up. Pre-filled with the FaceGate fare gates scenario so the flow reads.

Tokens & red-line cards

Used only in Converge. Each participant gets one set of gradient tokens and places one on each tension point (not each category). Red-line cards are laid on a tension point to force a redesign — or they document a "no."

Gradient-of-agreement tokens — one set per participant
Print one row per participant. Place on tension points, after deliberation — never during disclosure or surfacing.
Red-line cards — the block that must be resolved
A red line on a tension point can't be tokened over. Resolve it with a condition/redesign, or it lands the outcome on "No — or not yet."
