DTPR Agreement Canvas

The workshop counterpart to the disclosure canvas. Where the disclosure board records a system that's already decided, this mat is what a neighborhood and a city/deployer fill in together — recording not a yes/no vote but the texture of agreement, the conditions attached to it, and where they couldn't agree.

Print & play. Two sheets on A4 / Letter. Sheet 1 is the mat (enlarge to A3/tabloid for the table if you can). Sheet 2 cuts up into gradient tokens and red-line cards. The mat here is pre-filled with a sample system (FaceGate fare gates) so the structure reads — in a real workshop the disclosure seats start face-down and the deployer flips them one at a time.

On screen, the five disclosure seats show a face-down cover — click one to “disclose” it. That’s the flip-to-disclose rule: the group can’t place agreement tokens on a seat until the deployer has revealed it.
