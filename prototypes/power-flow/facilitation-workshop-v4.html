Provocation deck — the "what if"

Each group randomly draws ~2. In Surface: "what new worry does this raise?" In Deliberate: "does your condition survive this?" Family = the color band; carry-tier = the corner badge.

Deployer event Vendor event Community context Upside
Carry model: core — print once, every scenario · pattern — same slot, reflavor per system · scenario — write fresh with the community. The scenario cards are the deck that must be built with a neighbourhood, not about one.

Redesign cards — turn a fix into a condition

Played in Deliberate to resolve a tension. Pick a printed pattern or hand-write your own on a blank. Whatever's played becomes that tension point's candidate condition on the mat.

Role tokens & red-line cards

Used only in Converge. Each participant takes one token in their seat's color and places it in the level-zone (endorse → block) that matches their view. The color shows who — the split is the signal.

Seat tokens — one per participant, in their role color
Print enough of each color for the seats in the room. Placed on tension points in Converge only — never during disclosure or surfacing.
Red-line cards — the block that must be resolved
Resolve with a redesign card, or it lands the outcome on "Proposal withdrawn / paused."
