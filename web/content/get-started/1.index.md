# What Is DTPR

DTPR (Digital Trust for Places and Routines) is an open standard for describing how technologies work in a clear, accessible way. It does this by highlighting the key questions our community research has shown people need answered to feel like they can trust how a technology is being used. Rather than relying on dense technical specifications or abstract terms of service agreements, DTPR translates these layers into simple language and visual iconography.

DTPR works by combining three core concepts together: a taxonomy of technology concepts, a visual language for describing a technology, and a mechanism for the public to give feedback on how a technology’s being used.
