DTPR was developed through co-design with residents, and workshops are still one of the ways it is used. In participatory co-design workshops, local communities test whether DTPR's signs and prompts make sense in the places they describe.

Workshops can center a community's needs in how technology is developed and overseen. Deployers have also used the [feedback survey](/docs/deployers/feedback-survey) questions in workshops and in-person interviews.
